FAQ

Questions

When can I download it?

Now. SayItSo is free on the App Store and Google Play.

What can I do for free?

For free you get 2 sets with up to 10 affirmations each, the Heart and Michael voices, your own voice, reminders, widgets and your 100-day flower, with no ads. Unlimited sets, every voice, photo backgrounds, background sounds, the sleep timer, focus view and listening with your screen off are part of Premium, which comes with a 7-day free trial.

Does it really work?

SayItSo is a tool for a small, kind daily habit. Saying encouraging words out loud can feel good, but it doesn't promise any particular result.

Are my affirmations private?

Yes. Your recordings never leave your phone, and shared garden cards show only your flowers and streak, never your words.
